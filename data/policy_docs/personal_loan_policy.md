# Personal & Auto Loan Underwriting Policy (Sample)

*This is illustrative sample policy content for demo purposes only — not real bank policy.*

## Section 1: Credit Score Thresholds

### 1.1 Minimum credit score
Applicants must have a credit score of at least 640 to be eligible for a
standard personal loan. Applicants with a score between 600–639 may be
approved only under the "near-prime" exception in Section 4.2, subject to a
higher interest rate tier.

### 1.2 Score-based amount caps
- Credit score 750+: eligible for loans up to $50,000 without additional collateral.
- Credit score 700–749: eligible for loans up to $30,000.
- Credit score 640–699: eligible for loans up to $15,000.
- Below 640: refer to manual underwriting; do not auto-approve.

## Section 2: Debt-to-Income (DTI) Requirements

### 2.1 Maximum DTI
Standard approval requires a debt-to-income ratio (existing debt payments
divided by annual income) below 0.40. Applications with DTI between 0.40
and 0.50 must be flagged for manual review and require a co-signer or
additional collateral. Applications with DTI above 0.50 must be rejected
outright, regardless of credit score.

### 2.2 DTI and loan purpose interaction
For auto loans specifically, DTI thresholds may be relaxed by 0.05 if the
vehicle itself serves as collateral, since recovery risk is lower.

## Section 3: Employment & Income Verification

### 3.1 Minimum employment history
Applicants must show at least 2 years of continuous employment history, or
1 year in the case of a documented career change within the same industry.

### 3.2 Income stability
Self-employed applicants require 2 years of tax returns as income
verification and are subject to a 10% haircut on stated income for risk
scoring purposes, to account for income volatility.

## Section 4: Exceptions & Manual Review Triggers

### 4.1 Automatic referral triggers
Any of the following automatically routes an application to manual
underwriting, bypassing auto-approval regardless of other factors:
- Loan amount exceeds $40,000
- Applicant has a bankruptcy filing within the last 7 years
- DTI between 0.40 and 0.50

### 4.2 Near-prime exception
Applicants with a credit score of 600–639 may still be approved if: (a)
DTI is below 0.30, (b) employment history exceeds 3 years, and (c) the loan
purpose is auto or debt consolidation. Interest rate tier is increased by
1.5 percentage points under this exception.

## Section 5: Loan Purpose Restrictions

### 5.1 Restricted purposes
Loans may not be approved for the purpose of purchasing cryptocurrency,
funding gambling activity, or refinancing another institution's loan that
is currently in default.

### 5.2 Preferred purposes
Debt consolidation and auto purchase applications receive a 0.5 percentage
point rate discount when DTI is below 0.35, as these purposes are
associated with lower historical default rates in the bank's portfolio.
